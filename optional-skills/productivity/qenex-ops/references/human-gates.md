# Human gates — QENEX LTD ops loop

The tick script will classify, draft, and queue. It will not replace a UK director.

## Always human

- Companies House filings, confirmation statements, VAT/HMRC
- Bank, wire, IBAN/SWIFT, paying or collecting invoices
- Patent filings (including GB2613981.6) and contract/NDA signature
- Sending any email, posting any public reply, publishing any price
- Spending (ads, hires, GPUs, model subscriptions, VPS)

## Agent may

- Classify inbound JSON dropped into `$HERMES_HOME/qenex-ops/inbox/`
- Draft Lab license / verifier / Pulse-archive-redirect replies on disk
- Refuse spend attempts into `refused/`

## Product

QENEX Lab is the only product. Pulse is archived. Do not reopen a Pulse sales motion.
